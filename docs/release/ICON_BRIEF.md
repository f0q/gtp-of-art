# Icon brief for a designer: GTP of Art

## About the app (short description)

GTP of Art stands for "Guess the Price of Art". It is an elegant iPhone game: the player sees a real
artwork (Old Masters, Impressionists, Japanese prints, vintage posters, early photographs) and guesses
what it sold for at auction. Six modes: pick the price, which is pricier, category quiz, bid with a
slider, swipe to build a taste profile, and a daily challenge. Free, offline, no ads, no accounts.
Audience: art-curious people 18–40 and casual trivia players. Tone: quiet luxury, a museum after hours,
an auction room; playful but never cartoonish.

## Brief for the icon / logo

Design the App Store icon (and a matching wordmark lock-up) for "GTP of Art", a game about guessing
auction prices of famous artworks.

Mood: "Salon Noir". Warm near-black gallery wall, one object lit as if under a picture light, gilt used
sparingly for anything to do with value. Think Christie's catalogue, old-money museum, brass picture
light on a dark wall. Minimal, instantly readable at 60 px.

Concept directions (pick one or propose better):
1. A gilt italic "G" set like a monogram on a dark wall, lit from above by a brass picture light.
2. An empty gilt frame on a dark wall with a small price tag or lot number hanging from one corner.
3. An auctioneer's gavel resting on a block, drawn as one clean gilt silhouette.

Palette (exact values, from the app's design system):
- Background #0E0D0C (warm near-black) or #1A1816 (warm charcoal)
- Gilt #C9A55C (main accent); a lighter highlight may be used for the lit edge
- Cream #F4EFE6 if a second light tone is needed
No other hues. No gradients that look like glass or neon.

Typography reference: the app uses Fraunces (a soft, slightly old-style serif) for display text and
San Francisco for interface text. If letters appear in the icon, use a serif with that character.

Must:
- Work as a single flat square, 1024 × 1024 px, no transparency, no rounded corners (iOS masks it).
- Keep the main shape inside the central 80 % so the mask does not clip it.
- Read clearly at 60 px and in grayscale. One focal object, generous negative space.
- Look right in iOS 26 Liquid Glass tinted and dark icon modes, so supply the icon as layered vector
  (background, object, highlight) if possible.
- Deliver: 1024 px PNG, SVG or layered Figma/Illustrator source, and a horizontal wordmark
  "GTP of Art" with the subtitle "Guess the Price of Art".

Must not:
- No reference to GPT, ChatGPT, OpenAI, robots, chips, chat bubbles or anything that reads "AI".
  The name is an initialism of Guess The Price; the icon should say "art and auctions", nothing else.
- No real artwork or recognisable painting, no museum logos, no auction-house logos.
- No dollar signs or price tags with real numbers, no coins, no money imagery.
- No emoji style, no 3D plastic, no clipart gavel.
- Never shorten the name to "GTP" on its own in the wordmark.

Reference material: three in-house sketches of the directions above are in the project at
tools/out/icon/ (icon-1-monogram.png, icon-2-frame.png, icon-3-gavel.png). They are mood references,
not finished work.
