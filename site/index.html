Guess the Price of Art

A Klimt, a Hokusai print, a Toulouse-Lautrec poster. Each one sold, and the figure is on public record. How close can you get?

Coming soon to the App Store

Free. Offline. No ads, no accounts, no data collected.

Six ways to play

  1. 01

    Pick the price

    Four prices, one is real. The wrong ones are close enough to make you think.

  2. 02

    Which is pricier?

    Two works, one sold for more. Trust your eye and keep the streak alive.

  3. 03

    Category quiz

    Baroque, Fauvism or Ukiyo-e? Name the movement and learn its tells.

  4. 04

    Bid

    Slide to your own estimate, from a thousand dollars to a billion.

  5. 05

    Daily challenge

    Five lots a day, the same for everyone. Share your result as a grid.

  6. 06

    Swipe

    Keep or pass through a stack of works. The app names your taste.
